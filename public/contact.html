Support

Frequently asked questions

Clear answers for hiring managers evaluating recruitment partnerships. If you don't see your question, reach out to our team directly.

How long does the typical placement take?

Most roles are filled within 2–4 weeks for professional positions and 48–72 hours for temporary staffing needs. Timelines vary by industry, seniority, and market conditions, but our proactive talent pools and pre-vetted candidate networks consistently deliver faster results than industry averages.

What industries do you serve?

We partner with organizations across technology, healthcare, finance, manufacturing, logistics, and professional services. Our specialized recruiters understand the nuances of each sector, from hard-to-find engineering talent to compliance-heavy healthcare staffing. Whether you need one critical hire or a full project team, our industry-aligned approach ensures cultural and technical fit.

How is your pricing structured?

We offer transparent, success-based pricing models: contingency search (pay only upon successful placement), retained search for executive roles, and contract/temporary staffing with straightforward hourly or daily rates. There are no hidden fees — you'll receive a clear proposal tailored to your hiring volume and role complexity before we begin.

What makes recruit3 different from other temporary staffing agencies?

Unlike traditional temporary staffing services, we combine data-driven candidate matching with a dedicated account team that learns your culture, not just your job descriptions. Our average client stays with us for 3+ years because we prioritize retention and quality over volume. Every candidate is interviewed, reference-checked, and skills-assessed before submission — saving your hiring managers hours of screening time.

Do you offer temporary-to-permanent hiring?

Yes. Our temp-to-perm model is one of our most popular solutions. You can evaluate a candidate's performance and cultural fit on the job before making a permanent offer. This approach significantly reduces mis-hire risk — over 85% of our temp-to-perm placements convert to full-time employees within six months.

What geographic regions do you cover?

We operate across North America and Europe, with dedicated regional teams who understand local talent markets, employment regulations, and salary benchmarks. Whether you're hiring for a single office or multiple locations, our coordinated approach ensures consistent quality and compliance across borders.
