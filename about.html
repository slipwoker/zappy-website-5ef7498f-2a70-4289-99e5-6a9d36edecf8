Our Story

How recruit3 was founded

We set out to solve slow, opaque hiring with a structured, transparent approach that puts people first.

  1. 2019

    The problem becomes clear

    After years in HR leadership, our founders saw the same pattern everywhere: hiring teams drowning in resumes, candidates lost in black holes, and critical roles staying vacant for months. The industry was built on speed over quality, and nobody was measuring what actually mattered — retention, culture fit, and long-term performance.

  2. 2020

    Building the blueprint

    We spent a year designing a recruitment methodology that combined structured interviewing, skills-based assessments, and transparent reporting dashboards. Every step was documented, every metric was tracked. We partnered with three early-adopter companies to pressure-test the model across tech, healthcare, and professional services hiring.

  3. 2021

    recruit3 launches

    We opened our doors with a clear mission: make hiring measurable. Our structured process reduced time-to-hire by 40% for our first ten clients while improving candidate quality scores. Word spread quickly among HR directors who were tired of the traditional agency model and wanted a true talent partner.

  4. 2022

    Scaling with integrity

    Our team grew to 25 recruitment specialists, each trained in our proprietary evaluation framework. We expanded into temporary staffing services and executive search, always maintaining the same level of rigor and transparency. Client retention hit 94%, and our placement success rate climbed above 92%.

  5. 2024

    Shaping the future of work

    Today, recruit3 partners with over 150 organizations across North America, from fast-growing startups to Fortune 500 enterprises. We continue to invest in data-driven hiring tools, diversity sourcing strategies, and the belief that every great company is built one great hire at a time.
